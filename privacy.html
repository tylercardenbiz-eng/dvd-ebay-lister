DVD eBay Lister Privacy Policy This application is used privately for managing and pricing physical media inventory for sale on eBay. The application may access eBay account information and marketplace data through eBay APIs as authorized by the user. Authentication credentials, refresh tokens, inventory information, and application data are stored locally on the user's computer and are not sold, shared, or distributed to third parties. eBay data is used only for inventory management, product identification, pricing research, and listing-related functions. Users may revoke the application's access to their eBay account at any time through their eBay account settings. Contact: tylercardenbiz@gmail.com
